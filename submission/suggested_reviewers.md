# Suggested reviewers

Editorial Manager asks for suggested reviewers. The people below are the authors of the work this
manuscript builds on or argues with, so they are the ones competent to judge it. They are listed
with the paper that makes them relevant, and nothing else.

**Affiliations and email addresses are deliberately omitted.** They change, and asserting a current
institution or address for a real person without checking it is exactly the kind of error that
should not appear in a submission. Look each person up before entering them in the system, and drop
anyone with whom there is a conflict of interest.

| Reviewer | Why they are relevant |
|---|---|
| Antoine Godichon-Baggioni | Author of the inversion-free streaming Newton method this paper extends (JMLR 26(59), 2025, with N. Werge) and of the online asymptotic-variance estimator under independent data (JSPI 203:1–19, 2019). Best placed to judge whether the extension to dependent data is correct and whether the credit boundaries are drawn honestly. |
| Nicklas Werge | Co-author of the same base method; same expertise on the curvature recursion and its cost accounting. |
| Wei Biao Wu | Long-run variance estimation and recursive estimation of time-average variance constants (Ann. Appl. Probab. 19(4), 2009); also a co-author of recent work on de-biased covariance estimation in SGD. Best placed to judge the block-gradient variance estimator and its rate. |
| Dootika Vats | Lugsail/flat-top lag windows for time-average covariance matrices (Biometrika 109(3), 2022), the correction whose bias order this paper uses at block-gradient resolution. |
| Sen Na | Online covariance matrix estimation in sketched Newton methods (Information and Inference 15(2), 2026): the closest prior work on inference for streaming second-order methods under independent data. |
| Xi Chen | Statistical inference for SGD, and inference under phi-mixing data (arXiv:2302.12717): directly adjacent on the dependent-data inference axis. |
| Eric Moulines | Non-asymptotic analysis of stochastic approximation and the stability theory (with Andrieu and Priouret) that the manuscript cites as the route to closing its one conditional proposition. |
