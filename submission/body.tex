\section*{Data availability}
All code, saved results and derived data needed to reproduce every number, figure and table in this
article are archived and citable \citep{kendiukhov2026bgsn}. The release used to produce the results
reported here is
\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21891313}{\texttt{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21891313}};
the concept identifier \texttt{10.5281/zenodo.21891312} always resolves to the most recent release,
and development continues at
\url{https://github.com/Kendiukhov/streaming-quasi-newton-dependent-data}. The archive contains the
estimator, the experiment scripts, the result files from which every reported number is generated,
and the tests that validate every closed-form population quantity against long simulation.

The two real data sets analysed are third-party and are not redistributed. They are downloaded from
the UCI Machine Learning Repository by \texttt{experiments/fetch\_data.py} and are cited in the
reference list: Metro Interstate Traffic Volume \citep{hogue2019metro}, \texttt{10.24432/C5X60B};
and Beijing Multi-Site Air Quality \citep{chen2019beijingairuci}, \texttt{10.24432/C5RK5G}.
